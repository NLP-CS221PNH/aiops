# P0230 — SPLADE: Sparse Lexical and Expansion Model for First Stage Ranking

**Tác giả:** Thibault Formal; Benjamin Piwowarski; Stéphane Clinchant. **Năm trích dẫn:** 2021. **Venue:** Proceedings of the 44th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Research and Development in Information Retrieval.

**Đã đọc:** Abstract. Mức đọc `abstract_only`; không đại diện cho systematic review toàn văn.

| Trường extraction | Nội dung |
|---|---|
| Tác vụ | Learned sparse first-stage retrieval |
| Observations / labels | Query/document lexical vocabulary representations. |
| Phương pháp | SPLADE dùng sparsity regularization và log-saturation term weights; dùng inverted index. |
| Evaluation / protocol | Trade-off effectiveness/efficiency khi thay regularization. |
