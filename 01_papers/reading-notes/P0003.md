# P0003 — Nissist: An Incident Mitigation Copilot based on Troubleshooting Guides

**Tác giả:** Kaikai An; Fangkai Yang; Junting Lu; Liqun Li; Zhixing Ren; Hao Huang; Lu Wang; Pu Zhao; Yu Kang; Hua Ding; Qingwei Lin; Saravan Rajmohan; Dongmei Zhang; Qi Zhang. **Năm trích dẫn:** 2024. **Venue:** Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence and Applications; ECAI 2024.

**Đã đọc:** Abstract; §2.1; §3 User Experiments. Mức đọc `fulltext_selected_sections`; không đại diện cho systematic review toàn văn.
